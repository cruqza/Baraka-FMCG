# Baraka Manufacturers — Dashboard Analysis

**Author:** Erickson Nyaga   
**Date:** 13 November 2026  


## Overview

This project analyses the **Baraka Manufacturers Dashboard** (`Baraka.pbix`), a Power BI dashboard that tracks sales, profits, discounting, and target performance across 7 East African markets. The analysis answers three business questions:

1. **Which markets are behind target, and since when?**
2. **Which months are strongest and weakest each year?**
3. **Where is discounting costing the most margin?**


## Key Findings at a Glance

### 1. Markets behind target
- All 7 markets are behind target; shortfall is chronic, not cyclical.
- Every market has been in consecutive monthly misses since **January 2024**.
- Kigali is closest to target (−0.004%); Dar es Salaam is worst (−0.16%).
- The 2024 calendar year was a complete miss (12/12 months).

### 2. Strongest / weakest months
- **January = weakest every year**; **December = strongest every year**.
- Seasonal spread is widening: ~3.9M (2023) → ~4.5M (2025).
- A reliable planning signal: January trough, December peak.

### 3. Discounting and margin
- No-discount margin: **31.0%** → High discount margin: **13.8%** (a 17.2 pp loss).
- Most margin lost by segment: **Government (−14.6 pp)**.
- Most margin lost by product: **Maize Flour (−18.2 pp)** and **Bar Soap (−18.0 pp)**.
